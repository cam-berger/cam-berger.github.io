Work / CIPHER

CIPHER

Decision support for a leader handed a new operations order. It turns dense text into a map, a timeline, and a plan they can question.

Years
2025–2026 · Draper Laboratory
My role
Author. Research on representation and on augmenting human awareness
Written for
Multisensory Intelligence, Responsive Environments
Publication
To appear at I/ITSEC 2026

The problem

A leader handed a new order has to turn pages of doctrine-shaped text into a plan, often on the front line and always on a clock. The paperwork competes with the thinking.

What I built

A harness of tool servers around a language model. Given an order, it builds a map, a timeline, and a flowchart of the plan, checks its own output for hallucinations, and proposes how the plan could be carried out. The leader works from an interactive picture instead of a blank page.

Method

  1. 1

    Represent

    The order is normalized into a structured schema, then rendered as a map, a timeline, and a flow.

  2. 2

    Judge

    A language model scores each output against the source order and flags what it cannot trace.

  3. 3

    Refine

    An automated pipeline evaluates and improves the prompts, including the judge’s own when its metrics broke.

What I learned

Using a language model as the judge worked once its own prompts were refined. Recommendations in open-ended situations still need a person in the loop.

What a bad recommendation costs

A bad recommendation costs trust.

That is the safety problem. AI in these settings has to sit inside a bounded system where risk assessment is real, and every output has to be traceable and honest.

Where it goes next

Changing the abstraction and representation of the event, or of the order itself, to give a language model real situational awareness.

This page contains

The high-level idea, what the I/ITSEC paper makes public, and where the work is going.

This page does not contain

Draper proprietary design, code, or data, and no operational material. If a detail seems missing, it is missing on purpose.

Why this is here

For Multisensory Intelligence, this is the safety half of my work: keeping a model’s recommendations traceable and honest when a person has to act on them. For Responsive Environments, it is the human-facing layer that AI-Firmware’s sensing would feed.
